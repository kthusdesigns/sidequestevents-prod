🌿 WELLNESS EXCHANGE WEDNESDAYS 🌿  





Let's bring our local wellness community together!



Wellness Exchange Wednesdays is a monthly in-person gathering designed to connect wellness professionals, build meaningful relationships, share resources, and create opportunities to grow together.



Whether you're a chiropractor, massage therapist, acupuncturist, personal trainer, nutritionist, yoga instructor, mental health professional, esthetician, holistic practitioner, or another wellness provider, we'd love to have you join us!







We believe our community is stronger when we work together. By getting to know what other local professionals do, we can:

🌱 Build genuine relationships and friendships.

🤝 Expand our professional referral networks.

💡 Share ideas, resources, and opportunities.

🚀 Explore collaborations and cross-promotions.

❤️ Help more people discover the services available within our local wellness community.



This isn't your typical networking event. No pressure, no formal presentations, and no awkward elevator pitches. Just good people, meaningful conversations, and opportunities to connect.



📅 EVENT DETAILS

Wednesday, December 2nd 

⏰ 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM  

📍 Atlas Coffee  

6504 S Broadway  Littleton, CO 80121



☕ Grab a coffee, introduce yourself, share what you do, and get to know some of the amazing people working to make our community healthier.



WHO SHOULD ATTEND?



Anyone involved in health, wellness, fitness, personal development, or helping people live healthier lives!



Feel free to invite another local wellness professional who would be a great addition to the group.



Our mission is simple: Good People. Greater Impact.



Let's connect, support one another, and raise the frequency of our wellness community! We look forward to seeing you there! 🌿
