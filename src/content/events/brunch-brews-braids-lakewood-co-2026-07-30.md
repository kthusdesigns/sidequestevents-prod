Join us for Brews & Braids, a hands-on braiding class for parents taught by a licensed hair stylist, hosted at Great Divide Brewery & Roadhouse in Lone Tree. Whether you've always wrestled with a simple ponytail or you're ready to level up to a French braid, this class meets you exactly where you are — no experience required.



You'll leave with real skills you can use the next morning before school drop-off.



What's included:

90-minute hands-on braiding instruction with a licensed stylist

1 Great Divide craft beer or non-alcoholic beverage - brunch can be ordered individually throughout the event

All practice supplies (combs, elastics, clips, sectioning tools)

A take-home kit with everything you need to keep practicing

Step-by-step technique guide to take home

What you'll learn

How to prep and section hair cleanly

The classic 3-strand braid (perfected)

French braid fundamentals

Easy everyday styles for school mornings

Who's this for?

Any parent, grandparent, guardian, or caregiver who wants to feel more confident doing their child's hair. Kids are welcome to come as your practice model, or you can work on a mannequin head (provided).



Good to know:

Spots are limited — grab yours before they're gone

21+ to drink; all ages welcome

Comfortable, casual atmosphere — this is meant to be fun!

Located at Great Divide's BelMar Taproom and Roadhouse 7260 W Alaska Dr, Lakewood, CO 80226
