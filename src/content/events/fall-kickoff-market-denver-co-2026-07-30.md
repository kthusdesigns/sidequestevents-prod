**Fall Kickoff Market at Great Divide RiNo**



Crisp mornings, patio afternoons, and Colorado’s favorite season have arrived.



Celebrate the beginning of fall with an afternoon at Great Divide Brewing Co. – RiNo, where local shopping, craft beer, live music, and community come together in one of Denver’s most vibrant neighborhoods.



Browse a thoughtfully curated market featuring talented Colorado artists, makers, vintage curators, and small businesses while enjoying seasonal food and drink specials from Great Divide. Grab a pint, wander the market, discover one-of-a-kind finds, relax on the brewery patio, and enjoy live local music throughout the afternoon.



With lawn games, cozy fall décor, and a welcoming atmosphere, the Fall Kickoff Market is the perfect excuse to slow down, spend time with friends, and support the incredible local businesses that make our community thrive.



Whether you’re looking for handcrafted gifts, unique home décor, artisan foods, or simply a great Saturday afternoon in RiNo, you’ll find plenty to explore.



**What You’ll Find:**



\* Curated market featuring Colorado artists, makers, vintage vendors, and small businesses

\* Seasonal food and drink specials from Great Divide Brewing Co.

\* Live local music

\* Lawn games on the patio

\* Cozy early-fall atmosphere

\* Family-friendly event

\* Dog-friendly outdoor spaces

\* Free admission



Bring your friends, grab your favorite fall beverage, shop local, and help us kick off another beautiful Colorado autumn in RiNo.
