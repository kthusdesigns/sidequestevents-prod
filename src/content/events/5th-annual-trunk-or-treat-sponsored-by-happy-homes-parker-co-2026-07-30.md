Happy Homes Neighborhood Trunk or Treat and Carnival at Looking Glass

Get ready for one of Looking Glass Neighborhood’s favorite fall traditions!

Bring the whole family for an afternoon of Halloween fun at the Happy Homes Neighborhood Trunk or Treat and Carnival at Looking Glass. This free community celebration combines the excitement of a classic trunk-or-treat with the energy of a neighborhood fall festival.

Stroll through rows of creatively decorated trunks as children collect candy from generous local families, then stick around to enjoy a festive carnival featuring games, attractions, music, and activities for all ages. Whether your little ones love showing off their costumes, competing in carnival games, or simply filling their treat bags, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

This event is designed to bring neighbors together, create lasting memories, and celebrate the season in a safe, welcoming environment.

Event Highlights

🎃 Decorated trunks filled with candy

🍬 Safe trick-or-treating for kids

🎡 Carnival games and family-friendly attractions

🎶 Music throughout the event

👻 Costume Contest with fun prizes!

📸 Plenty of festive photo opportunities

🍁 A fun evening celebrating community and fall
