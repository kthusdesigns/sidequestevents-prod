**Dry Creek Fall Festival!**

Crisp mornings, sunny afternoons, and Colorado’s favorite season have arrived.

Celebrate the beginning of fall with a beautiful afternoon in Centennial, where local shopping, live entertainment, family fun, and community come together for the Dry Creek Fall Festival.

Browse a thoughtfully curated market featuring talented Colorado artists, makers, local businesses, and unique vendors while enjoying everything the festival has to offer. Wander the market, discover one-of-a-kind finds, grab something delicious to eat, and spend the afternoon enjoying the Colorado fall weather with family and friends.

With activities for the kids, entertainment, local vendors, and plenty to explore, the Dry Creek Fall Festival is the perfect excuse to get outside, connect with neighbors, and support the incredible small businesses that make our community thrive.



Whether you’re looking for handcrafted gifts, unique home décor, artisan goods, something tasty to eat, or simply a fun way to spend a fall Sunday in Centennial, you’ll find plenty to enjoy.



What You’ll Find:

Curated market featuring Colorado artists, makers, vendors, and small businesses

Food and treats from local vendors

Live entertainment

Family-friendly activities

Festive fall atmosphere

Plenty of opportunities to shop and support local

Family-friendly event

Free admission



Bring your family, invite your friends, shop local, and join us as we celebrate another beautiful Colorado autumn together at the Dry Creek Fall Festival in Centennial.
