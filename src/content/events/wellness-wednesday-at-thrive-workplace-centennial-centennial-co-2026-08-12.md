# Wellness Wednesday

### Your workday could use a wellness break.

Join us at **Thrive Workplace Centennial on Wednesday, August 26 from 12–3 PM** for Wellness Wednesday—an afternoon of movement, relaxation, recovery, and local wellness resources, all conveniently brought into the workplace.

Take a yoga break, loosen up with a massage therapist, learn about IV hydration, connect with local wellness professionals, and discover a few new ways to take better care of yourself without having to sacrifice your entire afternoon.

**Come for one experience or check out everything.** Wellness Wednesday is designed to be easy, approachable, and flexible enough to fit between meetings, deadlines, and everything else on your calendar.

### What You'll Find

**Yoga**
Move, stretch, breathe, and reset.

**Massage Therapy**
Take a few minutes to relieve tension and give your shoulders a break from your desk.

**IV Hydration**
Connect with an IV hydration provider and learn more about hydration and wellness options.

**Plus More**
Meet local wellness professionals, explore resources, and discover services designed to help you feel your best.
