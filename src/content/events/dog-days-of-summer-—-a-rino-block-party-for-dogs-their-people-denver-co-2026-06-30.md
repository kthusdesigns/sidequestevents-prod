The sun's out, the patio's open, and your pup is officially invited. Dog Days of Summer brings the SideQuest spirit to RiNo for an afternoon of good beer, good boys (and girls), and good vibes at Backyard on Blake.

Bring your best friend, leash up, and let's Party Up Denver-style.

📍 Backyard on Blake — 3040 Blake St, Denver, CO 80205

📅 Saturday, August 1 — 12PM–4PM

🎟️ Free to attend

What's Waiting for You:



Live DJ spinning all afternoon

Local vendors to browse

Food & drink specials (humans welcome too)

Water stations & dog treats for the four-legged crew

Giveaways all day long



Proudly sponsored by Great Divide Brewing Co. — because great beer and great dogs just go together.

This Quest is free, but the good vibes are limited — bring your crew and claim your spot on the patio.
