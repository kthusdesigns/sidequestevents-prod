Sunday Yoga for Body, Mind & Soul



Start your Sunday with movement, fresh air, and good energy. Yoga in the Park is a donation-based outdoor series open to all levels — whether you're brand new to yoga or you've been practicing for years, you're welcome here.



When: Sundays — July 12, July 26 & August 2 at 9:00 AM

Where: Clement Park, near the amphitheater

Cost: Donation-based — give what feels good



Bring your mat and props, bring a friend, bring your smile — and leave feeling refreshed. All levels welcome. Come as you are.
