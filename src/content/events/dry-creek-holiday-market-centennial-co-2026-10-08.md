Dry Creek Holiday Market



Shop Small. Gift Big. Celebrate Local.



Get into the holiday spirit at the Dry Creek Holiday Market! Join us for a festive day of shopping, community, and holiday cheer featuring 40+ local vendors, makers, artists, and small businesses from across Colorado.



Whether you're searching for the perfect handmade gift, treating yourself to something special, or simply looking for a fun holiday outing with family and friends, this is the place to be!



Discover unique gifts, handcrafted goods, seasonal favorites, and one-of-a-kind finds you won't see at the big-box stores. Every purchase is an opportunity to support a local entrepreneur and make someone's holiday a little brighter.



Event Details



Sunday, December 13, 2026

11:00 AM – 4:00 PM

7200 E. Dry Creek Road, Centennial, CO



🎄 FREE ADMISSION — Everyone is welcome!



What to Expect



40+ Local Vendors: Shop a curated selection of Colorado small businesses, artisans, and makers.



Unique Holiday Gifts: Find handmade treasures, creative gifts, home décor, accessories, and more.



Festive Holiday Atmosphere: Enjoy a welcoming, community-centered shopping experience that's perfect for getting into the holiday spirit.



Family-Friendly Fun: Bring your friends, family, and holiday shopping list!



Support Small Businesses: Keep your holiday shopping dollars local while discovering some incredible Colorado entrepreneurs.



Make Your Holiday Shopping Mean More



The best gifts don't always come from a big store. Sometimes they're handmade, thoughtfully created, and sold by the person who poured their heart into making them.



This holiday season, skip the ordinary and discover something extraordinary at the Dry Creek Holiday Market.



Admission is completely free, so bring the whole crew and celebrate the season with us!
