Before we trade patio weather for pumpkin spice, let’s celebrate one more incredible Colorado summer together.



Join us at Great Divide Brewing Co. – RiNo for an afternoon filled with local shopping, cold craft beer, delicious food and drink specials, yard games, and the laid-back community atmosphere that makes the RiNo neighborhood one of Denver’s favorite destinations.



Spend the afternoon exploring a curated market featuring talented Colorado artists, makers, and small businesses while enjoying live music, relaxing on the brewery patio, and discovering something new around every corner.



Whether you’re looking for a unique gift, meeting friends for a beer, supporting local businesses, or simply searching for the perfect Saturday outing, there’s something for everyone.

What You’ll Find



\* Curated local artisan and maker market

\* Great Divide food and drink specials

\* Live music throughout the afternoon

\* Yard games on the patio

\* Family-friendly atmosphere

\* Dog-friendly outdoor spaces

\* Plenty of opportunities to shop local and support Colorado small businesses

Admission is FREE, so invite your friends, grab a pint, and help us send summer off the right way.

We’ll see you in RiNo!
