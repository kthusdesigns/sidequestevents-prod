ABOUT SIDEQUEST EVENTS EVENT PLANNING & PRODUCTION COMPANY

SideQuest Events is a Centennial-based event planning and production company serving Denver and South Metro Denver. From community festivals and vendor markets to private celebrations, we plan and produce gatherings that give people a real reason to show up.

Our Story Why We Started Throwing Community Events

The name SideQuest Events comes from gaming culture. The best moments in any story tend to happen when you step off the beaten path. South Metro Denver’s local makers, small businesses, and community connectors tend to exist a little off the main road. We build events that put them front and center.
